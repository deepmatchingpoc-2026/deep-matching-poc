# Deep Matching (proof of concept)

Academic, non-commercial proof of concept developed by a team of students
of the Executive Master in AI for Business, Bologna Business School.

The project explores matching people based on *how* they engage with
culture (films, books), rather than *what* they consume. It will be shown
once to the course professors at the end of the Master. No paying users,
no advertising, no revenue. The demo uses synthetic (made-up) profiles only.

## Data sources
- Film and series metadata: TMDB API (cached locally for less than 6 months)
- Book metadata: Open Library API (cached locally)
